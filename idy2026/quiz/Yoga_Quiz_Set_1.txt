SET 1

1. What is the meaning of the word “Yoga”?
A) Exercise
B) Union
C) Meditation
D) Breathing
Answer: B) Union

2. Which yoga pose resembles a tree?
A) Bhujangasana
B) Tadasana
C) Vrikshasana
D) Vajrasana
Answer: C) Vrikshasana

3. How many Angas are there in Ashtanga Yoga?
A) 5
B) 6
C) 8
D) 4
Answer: C) 8

4. Who is known as the Father of Yoga?
A) Maharshi Patanjali
B) Shreenivas Bhat
C) Gorakshanath
D) Swami Swatmaram
Answer: A) Maharshi Patanjali

5. Which yoga posture is performed while sitting on the heels?
A) Vajrasana
B) Tadasana
C) Bhujangasana
D) Vrikshasana
Answer: A) Vajrasana

6. Which pranayama technique is generally associated with rapid breathing?
A) Anulom Vilom
B) Bhramari
C) Kapalbhati
D) Sheetali
Answer: C) Kapalbhati

7. Which yoga practice mainly focuses on withdrawal of senses?
A) Dhyana
B) Pratyahara
C) Asana
D) Samadhi
Answer: B) Pratyahara

8. Which asana resembles a bow shape?
A) Dhanurasana
B) Trikonasana
C) Tadasana
D) Vajrasana
Answer: A) Dhanurasana

9. Which ministry promotes International Yoga Day?
A) Ministry of Ayush
B) Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
C) Ministry of Culture
D) Ministry of Education
Answer: A) Ministry of Ayush

10. Which sequence is correct according to the eight limbs of yoga?
A) Dharana → Dhyana → Samadhi
B) Samadhi → Dhyana → Dharana
C) Dhyana → Asana → Yama
D) Asana → Pranayama → Yama
Answer: A) Dharana → Dhyana → Samadhi
