SET 3

1. When do we celebrate International Day of Yoga?
A) 21 June
B) 21 July
C) 11 June
D) 21 May
Answer: A) 21 June

2. What is the Sanskrit translation of the word Yoga?
A) To breathe
B) To unite or join
C) To stretch
D) To meditate
Answer: B) To unite or join

3. Chin lock means?
A) Moola Bandha
B) Uddiyana Bandha
C) Jalandhara Bandha
D) None of the above
Answer: C) Jalandhara Bandha

4. The term Bandhas means:
A) Lock
B) Posture
C) Gesture
D) None of the above
Answer: A) Lock

5. Which breathing technique is one of the Shatkarma cleansing kriyas?
A) Kapalbhati
B) Bhastrika
C) Sheetali
D) Bhramari
Answer: A) Kapalbhati

6. Which pose requires maximum balance?
A) Shavasana
B) Vrikshasana
C) Vajrasana
D) Makarasana
Answer: B) Vrikshasana

7. Which yoga practice is most directly linked with improving breath control?
A) Yama
B) Pranayama
C) Samadhi
D) Dharana
Answer: B) Pranayama

8. Which statement about yoga is MOST accurate?
A) Yoga only improves flexibility
B) Yoga is only for elderly people
C) Yoga benefits both mind and body
D) Yoga is purely a religious activity
Answer: C) Yoga benefits both mind and body

9. Which asana is performed lying flat on the back?
A) Shavasana
B) Bhujangasana
C) Vajrasana
D) Vrikshasana
Answer: A) Shavasana

10. Which statement best describes meditation?
A) A method to increase physical strength only
B) A technique to calm and focus the mind
C) A fast breathing exercise
D) A competitive activity
Answer: B) A technique to calm and focus the mind
