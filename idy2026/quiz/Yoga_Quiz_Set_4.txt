SET 4

1. Which of the following is NOT one of the 8 Limbs of Yoga (Ashtanga)?
A) Yama
B) Niyama
C) Shavasana
D) Pratyahara
Answer: C) Shavasana

2. The Sanskrit name Adho Mukha Svanasana is commonly known as:
A) Tree Pose
B) Mountain Pose
C) Cat & Cow Pose
D) Downward Dog Pose
Answer: D) Downward Dog Pose

3. Which prayer is chanted to express gratitude towards Mother Earth?
A) Opening Prayer
B) Shanti Path
C) Ending Prayer
D) Pruthwi Kshama Yachana
Answer: D) Pruthwi Kshama Yachana

4. While practicing Akara separately, which breathing technique is used?
A) Abdominal
B) Thoracic
C) Clavicular
D) None of the above
Answer: A) Abdominal

5. Vajrasana is also known as:
A) Cobra Pose
B) Thunderbolt Pose
C) Tree Pose
D) Bow Pose
Answer: B) Thunderbolt Pose

6. Which combination is most suitable for improving flexibility and relaxation?
A) Surya Namaskar + Meditation
B) Weightlifting + Sprinting
C) Running + Pushups
D) Cycling + Boxing
Answer: A) Surya Namaskar + Meditation

7. Which asana is generally NOT recommended immediately after a heavy meal?
A) Vajrasana
B) Shavasana
C) Surya Namaskar
D) Sukhasana
Answer: C) Surya Namaskar

8. In Surya Namaskar, the number of standard positions in one round is:
A) 8
B) 10
C) 12
D) 14
Answer: C) 12

9. In yoga philosophy, Niyama refers to:
A) Physical postures
B) Moral restraints
C) Personal observances
D) Breathing exercises
Answer: C) Personal observances

10. Which of the following is considered both a physical and mental discipline?
A) Chess only
B) Weight training only
C) Yoga
D) Sprinting only
Answer: C) Yoga
