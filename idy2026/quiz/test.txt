SET 5

1. What does the word "Pranayama" refer to?
A) Physical postures
B) Breath control
C) Meditation
D) Chanting
Answer: B) Breath control

2. Which Veda contains the earliest references to Yoga?
A) Rigveda
B) Samaveda
C) Yajurveda
D) Atharvaveda
Answer: A) Rigveda

3. What is the final stage of Ashtanga Yoga?
A) Dhyana
B) Samadhi
C) Dharana
D) Pratyahara
Answer: B) Samadhi

4. Which of the following is considered a balancing asana?
A) Shavasana
B) Garudasana
C) Makarasana
D) Balasana
Answer: B) Garudasana

5. How many chakras are commonly recognized in the human body?
A) 5
B) 7
C) 9
D) 11
Answer: B) 7

6. Which yoga text was compiled by Sage Patanjali?
A) Bhagavad Gita
B) Hatha Yoga Pradipika
C) Yoga Sutras
D) Upanishads
Answer: C) Yoga Sutras

7. What is the literal meaning of "Hatha"?
A) Sun and Moon
B) Body and Mind
C) Forceful
D) Light and Dark
Answer: C) Forceful

8. Which asana is known as the Cobra pose?
A) Bhujangasana
B) Matsyasana
C) Halasana
D) Chakrasana
Answer: A) Bhujangasana

9. In yoga, what is "Prana"?
A) Vital life force
B) Mental clarity
C) Physical strength
D) Emotional balance
Answer: A) Vital life force

10. What does "Mudra" mean in yoga?
A) A type of diet
B) A breathing exercise
C) A symbolic hand gesture
D) A chanting technique
Answer: C) A symbolic hand gesture
