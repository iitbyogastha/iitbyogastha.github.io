SET 2

1. How many steps are there in Surya Namaskar?
A) 11
B) 12
C) 10
D) 9
Answer: B) 12

2. Which asana is named after a triangle?
A) Trikonasana
B) Shalabhasana
C) Gomukhasana
D) Naukasana
Answer: A) Trikonasana

3. Which is the second chakra?
A) Anahata Chakra
B) Manipur Chakra
C) Swadhishthana Chakra
D) Ajna Chakra
Answer: C) Swadhishthana Chakra

4. Meaning of Hatha Yoga?
A) Balance of Surya & Chandra Nadi
B) Balance of Ida & Pingala
C) Both A & B
D) None of the above
Answer: C) Both A & B

5. The practice of controlling breath in yoga is called:
A) Yama
B) Niyama
C) Pranayama
D) Samadhi
Answer: C) Pranayama

6. Which asana primarily strengthens the spine while opening the chest?
A) Bhujangasana
B) Shavasana
C) Tadasana
D) Balasana
Answer: A) Bhujangasana

7. Which pranayama is often associated with cooling the body?
A) Bhastrika
B) Kapalbhati
C) Sheetali
D) Ujjayi
Answer: C) Sheetali

8. Which of the following is NOT one of the eight limbs of Yoga?
A) Dharana
B) Samadhi
C) Pratyahara
D) Zumba
Answer: D) Zumba

9. Which of the following is a meditative sitting posture?
A) Padmasana
B) Bhujangasana
C) Halasana
D) Chakrasana
Answer: A) Padmasana

10. Which term in yoga is most closely related to concentration?
A) Dharana
B) Asana
C) Yama
D) Niyama
Answer: A) Dharana
