Yogastha




About Us

Yogastha (meaning "established in yoga") is a Yoga and Wellness Club under the Students' Gymkhana at IIT Bombay. Since its inception on the 1st International Day of Yoga (21st June 2015), the club has been working towards creating a stress-free, peaceful campus community by addressing all aspects of wellness, including physical, emotional, spiritual, and intellectual well-being. The club actively conducts weekly sessions and other events to promote these goals. Our tagline: समत्वं योग उच्यते| (Samatvam Yog Uchyate: ‘Equanimity is Yoga’).

International Day of Yoga

IDY 2015

International Day of Yoga 2015

The Yoga Day of 2015 was special because Yogastha was officially launched. Activities included movie screenings and a Yogathon Challenge.

IDY 2017

International Day of Yoga 2017

IDY 2017 was celebrated with workshops, meditation sessions, and guest lectures by renowned yoga practitioners.

IDY 2018

International Day of Yoga 2018

A first-of-its-kind Yoga Week culminated in the practice of Common Yoga Protocol (CYP) and the Yogathon Challenge on the event day.

IDY 2019

International Day of Yoga 2019

The International Day of Yoga is celebrated on 21st June every year. In 2019, a full week of yoga from 14th to 21st June was celebrated, including movie screenings, lectures, practice sessions, and more.

IDY 2020

International Day of Yoga 2020

Due to the pandemic, IDY 2020 was conducted online, featuring virtual yoga sessions, webinars, and interactive discussions.

IDY 2021

International Day of Yoga 2021

The theme of IDY 2021 was "Yoga for Wellness," focusing on physical and mental well-being amidst challenging times.
